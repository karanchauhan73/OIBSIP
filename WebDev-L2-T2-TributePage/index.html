Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam

Biography

Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam was one of India's greatest scientists and the 11th President of India. He was born on 15 October 1931 in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu. Coming from a humble background, he worked hard to achieve his dreams and became an inspiration for millions of students.

He played a significant role in India's missile and space programs while working with ISRO and DRDO. Because of his contribution to missile technology, he earned the title "Missile Man of India."

As the President of India from 2002 to 2007, he became known as the "People's President." He believed that education, innovation, and hard work were the keys to building a developed nation.

Even after leaving the presidency, he continued teaching students and motivating young minds until his last breath. His life remains an example of honesty, dedication, and service to the nation.

Major Achievements

  1. 1931 – Born in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu.
  2. 1960 – Joined DRDO as a scientist.
  3. 1969 – Worked with ISRO on India's first Satellite Launch Vehicle.
  4. 1980 – Successfully launched the Rohini Satellite.
  5. 1998 – Played a major role in Pokhran-II Nuclear Tests.
  6. 2002–2007 – Served as the 11th President of India.
  7. 2015 – Passed away while delivering a lecture to students.
