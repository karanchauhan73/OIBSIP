# Tribute Page – Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam

A visually engaging tribute page dedicated to Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam, one of India's most respected scientists, educators, and former Presidents.

## 📌 Project Overview

This project is a responsive tribute page created to honor the life, achievements, contributions, and inspiring legacy of Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam.

The page presents a biography, important achievements, a timeline of major events, and an inspirational quote. The design focuses on creating a clean and visually engaging user experience using HTML5 and CSS3.

## 🛠️ Technologies Used

* HTML5
* CSS3

## ✨ Features

* Page title with Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam's name
* One-line inspirational tagline
* Prominent tribute image
* Biography section with original written content
* Timeline of important life events and achievements
* Notable quote displayed in a separate styled quote block
* Multiple background colors used across different sections
* Different font styles for headings and body text
* Responsive layout for different screen sizes
* Clean and user-friendly interface

5. Explore the tribute page.

## 👤 About the Subject

Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam was an Indian aerospace scientist and the 11th President of India. He played an important role in India's space and missile development programs and became widely known as the "People's President."

He was also a great educator and motivational speaker who inspired millions of students to dream big and work hard. His life and achievements continue to motivate young people across the world.
